    Infrared and visible image fusion (IVF) endeavors to
engineer composite outputs by blending optimal virtues
of divergent modalities. This paper reveals, unprecedent-
edly, the intrinsic “attention properties” of infrared images,
which directly arise from their physical characteristics (i.e.,                          (a)                          (b)
